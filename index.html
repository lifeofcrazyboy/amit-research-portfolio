01 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Where atmosphere
meets the cryosphere.

I combine high-altitude field measurements, remote sensing, and data analysis to study aerosols and their interactions with snow and ice.

01

Aerosols & snow darkening

Investigating mineral dust, black carbon, and their role in snow albedo reduction across the Western Himalayas.

PARTICLES → SNOW → CLIMATE
02

Ice-nucleating particles

Exploring how biomass-burning and mineral-dust events influence particles that initiate ice formation in clouds.

AEROSOLS → CLOUD MICROPHYSICS
03

Observations & analysis

Connecting aerosol optical and microphysical properties with satellite observations, trajectories, and environmental data.

03 / FIELDWORK & METHODS

Understanding starts
on the mountain.

From glacier treks to multi-season snow sampling, my work is grounded in direct observations of the Western Himalayas.

  • 01

    High-altitude campaigns

    Snow sampling, glacier expeditions, and in situ aerosol and snow-property measurements.

  • 02

    Instrumentation in challenging conditions

    Deploying and calibrating nephelometers, aethalometers, SMPS, OPC, and sun photometers.

  • 03

    From sampling to insight

    Field logistics and safety coordination, laboratory analysis, and integrated observational datasets.
